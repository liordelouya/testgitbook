# Overview

The Overview is the landing page of every assessment. It gives you a summary of where the assessment stands — what needs attention, what's been collected, and what's still missing — without having to navigate through each tab individually.

The right sidebar shows the **assessment details** (project, assignees, start date) and the **vendor profile** (inherent risk, lifecycle, and description) for quick reference.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If the vendor's Inherent Risk Profile hasn't been validated recently, a warning banner will appear at the top. Lema uses the IRQ to decide which controls to evaluate — review and validate it before proceeding. See Inherent Risk Profile.
{% endhint %}

### Issues

The Issues panel shows your current **Assessment Score** and a breakdown of unvalidated controls by priority:

* **Critical controls** — must be resolved before the assessment can be concluded as Acceptable
* **Medium-priority controls** — unvalidated controls with moderate weight
* **Low-priority controls** — unvalidated controls with lower weight

The score updates in real time as controls are validated. Click **Manage issues** to go directly to the Issues tab and work through them.

The **Evidence request** status shows whether a Smart Evidence Request has been published to the vendor. If not yet sent, it will show as _Not published_. See [Issues](issues.md) for details.

### Artifacts

A summary of all artifacts collected for this assessment — how many came from public sources and how many were manually uploaded. Click **Upload artifacts** to add documents directly from the overview. See [Artifacts](artifacts.md) for details.

### Questionnaires

Shows the number of questionnaires sent to the vendor for this assessment. Click **Send a questionnaire** to create and publish one. See [Questionnaires](questionnaires.md) for details.

### Controls

A summary of all controls in scope for this assessment, broken down by scope group (e.g. Business Continuity - Critical and Above, Privacy - High and Above). Click **View all controls** to open the full controls list. See [Controls](controls.md) for details.
