# Business Coverage

The Business Coverage Dashboard shows how much of your third-party portfolio is actually being overseen — broken down by risk level, assessment status, and compliance framework. Use it to identify blind spots and make the case for expanding your TPRM program.



**Third-Party Inherent Risk Distribution**

A donut chart showing your full vendor portfolio broken down by inherent risk level — Critical, High, Medium, Low — with counts and percentages. Use this to understand the risk composition of your inventory at a glance.

**Coverage Distribution**

A donut chart showing the percentage of third parties that have been assessed at least once. The breakdown on the right shows assessed counts per inherent risk tier, so you can see where coverage gaps are concentrated.

{% hint style="info" %}
Low coverage against your Critical and High tier vendors is the clearest signal that your program needs to prioritize. A vendor with no assessment history is an unknown risk.
{% endhint %}

**Compliance by Framework**

A horizontal bar chart showing how many vendors in your portfolio hold each certification — ISO 27001, ISO 27701, SOC 2, and others. Hover over any bar to see the vendor count and the percentage of your total inventory it represents.

**Compliance by Risk**

A breakdown of framework coverage by inherent risk tier. For each combination of risk level and framework (e.g. Critical vendors with SOC 2), you can see what percentage of that tier holds the certification. This surfaces where your highest-risk vendors have the weakest compliance footprint.
