# Risk

The Risk Dashboard gives you a real-time view of your organization's third-party risk posture — active threats, inventory composition, and assessment hygiene — in a single page.



**High and Critical Findings**

A live feed of the most recent High and Critical severity findings across your inventory. Each row shows the vendor, severity level, finding type, and how long ago it was detected. Use this to catch emerging threats without needing to search through the full Findings table.



**Open and Ongoing Findings over Time**

An area chart tracking Critical and High severity findings over the last 30 days. To the right, current counts are shown alongside a percentage change versus 30 days ago — so you can tell at a glance whether your exposure is growing or shrinking.



**Third-Parties with the Highest Risk**

A ranked bar chart of vendors by total open and ongoing findings, broken down by Critical (red) and High (orange). This surfaces which vendors are driving the most unresolved risk and helps you prioritize where to focus remediation efforts.



**Third-Parties per Inherent Risk**

A breakdown of your vendor inventory by inherent risk level — Critical, High, Medium, Low — compared to 30 days ago. The percentage change indicator next to each tier tells you whether your inventory is shifting toward higher or lower risk over time.



**Time Third-Parties Stay in 'Assessment Required'**

A gauge showing the average number of days vendors remain in **Assessment Required** or **Re-Assessment Required** status, measured against a configurable threshold.

If the average sits far above the threshold, it signals a backlog: vendors that need review are not getting assessed quickly enough. The count below the gauge shows how many vendors are currently overdue and the maximum days observed in the dataset.

{% hint style="info" %}
The threshold shown on the gauge is configured in your assessment cadence settings. Adjusting it changes what counts as "overdue" across the dashboard.
{% endhint %}
