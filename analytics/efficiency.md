# Efficiency

The Efficiency Dashboard tracks how quickly your team and vendors move through assessments — surfacing cycle time trends, vendor response behavior, and the share of controls being validated automatically.

### Summary Metrics

Four KPI cards at the top of the page give you an instant read on the current period:

* **Avg. completion time** — average days from assessment start to conclusion
* **Median completion time** — median days to complete, less sensitive to outliers than the average
* **Completed assessments** — total assessments concluded in the selected period
* **Automation** — percentage of controls validated automatically, without manual review

Each card shows a percentage change versus the prior period so you can tell immediately if things are improving or slipping.

### Charts View

**Assessment Cycle Time**

A combined bar and line chart showing weekly assessment volume (bars) alongside median cycle time (line). Hover over any week to see the number of assessments completed and the median days it took to close them.

The dashed segment at the end of the line represents the current incomplete week — treat it as directional, not final.

Below the chart: total completed assessments for the period and the overall median cycle time with period-over-period change.

**Third-Party Response Time Distribution**

A histogram bucketing assessments by how long vendors took to complete their questionnaire — from under 27 days to 197+ days. Use this to understand where vendor delays are concentrated and set realistic response time expectations.

The overall median response time is shown below the chart with a period-over-period change indicator.

**Assessment Automation**

A donut chart showing the percentage of controls validated automatically out of all controls assessed in the period. To the right, an area chart tracks the automation rate week over week — a rising trend means Lema's AI is covering more of your control validation over time.

Below the donut: total controls assessed and the raw count of automated validations.
