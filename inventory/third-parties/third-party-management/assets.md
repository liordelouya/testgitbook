# Assets

The Assets tab provides a view of the vendor's physical and personnel infrastructure as detected by Lema — including office locations and employee distribution across geographies.

Assets are grouped by **country** by default. Each asset shows its **type**, **tags**, and available **details**.

| Type          | What it represents                                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Personnel** | Employee headcount in a specific country or region, tagged by department |
| **Facility**  | Known office or operational location                                     |

### Why It Matters

Understanding where a vendor operates and where their employees are located is relevant for:

* **Data residency** — identifying which jurisdictions the vendor's personnel and infrastructure operate in
* **Concentration risk** — spotting over-reliance on a single geography
* **Regulatory exposure** — flagging operations in regions subject to specific data protection laws

### Filtering

Use the **Country** and **Tags** filters to focus on specific geographies or segments of the vendor's workforce and facilities.
