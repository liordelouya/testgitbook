# Findings

The Findings tab shows all risk findings detected by Lema for this vendor. Findings are generated by Lema's automated recon engine, which continuously scans publicly available information, vendor artifacts, and relationship data to surface risks that traditional assessments miss.

Learn more about [Findings](../../../monitoring/findings.md)

Each finding shows its **name**, **severity** (Critical, High, Medium, Low), **work status**, and **date detected**.

### Finding Detail

Click any finding to open its full detail view:

* **Description** — what was found and where
* **What is the Risk?** — the business impact if left unaddressed
* **Suggested Actions** — concrete remediation steps recommended by Lema
* **Evidence** — direct quotes from the source documents or data that triggered the finding, with a link to the original source
* **Control -** a finding can serve as an evidence for a control evaluation. In this case, the control name will appier in the finding drawer.&#x20;

### Managing Findings

From the finding drawer you can:

* **Change severity** — adjust the severity level if your context warrants it
* **Update work status** — track remediation progress (Open, In Progress, Resolved)
* **Hide finding** — remove it from the active view without marking it resolved
* **False Positive** — flag the finding as not applicable to this vendor

{% hint style="info" %}
Findings on this tab are scoped to this vendor. For a cross-vendor view of all findings across your inventory, see Findings.
{% endhint %}
