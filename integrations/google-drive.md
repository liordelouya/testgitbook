### Integration steps

#### Connect in Lema

1. Go to **Integrations → + Add Integration → Google Drive**.
2. Click **Grant Permissions**.
3. Choose the Google account you'd like to connect.
4. Select the specific folders you want Lema to access.
5. Click **Add Integration**.



Lema will start pulling shared document data from your selected folders right away.
